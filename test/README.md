- `index.html`: public-facing V6 Hybrid Editorial (no tape), responsive to viewport width; article reading and Aa demonstration.

- Test URLs: https://easylearnfromtw.github.io/signwellbio/test/ and https://easylearnfromtw.github.io/signwellbio/test/review.html

No live CMS APIs are called. Test screens are not real articles or business metrics. Do not treat demo success as production integration QA.
